Captain Cool of Indian Cricket

MS Dhoni

Introduction

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is one of the greatest cricketers and captains in the history of Indian cricket. His calm leadership, dedication, and finishing abilities have inspired millions of fans across the world.

Early Life

MS Dhoni was born on July 7, 1981, in Ranchi, Jharkhand. Before becoming a cricketer, he was interested in football and badminton. Through hard work and determination, he became one of India's most successful captains.

Major Achievements

ICC T20 World Cup Winner - 2007
ICC Cricket World Cup Winner - 2011
ICC Champions Trophy Winner - 2013
Multiple IPL Titles with Chennai Super Kings
Padma Bhushan Award Recipient
